# Bobby's Race Tools - Quick guide

## Get started

1. Open The Karters 2 and run **BobbyRaceTools.exe**.
2. Click **Connect**. Leave **Local player** on **Automatic**, or choose an active local player.
3. In **Camera**, enable **Camera overrides** and adjust your view. Settings save automatically.

## Camera during a race

- **F8**: show or hide Camera inside the game. You can keep driving while it is visible and adjust values with the mouse.
- **Ctrl+Tab** or controller **View/Back**: switch between driving and keyboard/controller editing.
- While editing, use **Tab/arrows** or **D-pad/stick** to navigate. Press **A** to select a slider, then the **D-pad** to adjust it. **Y** resets the focused slider.
- **B** or **Esc**: leave editing and return to driving, keeping the panel visible.
- Use a value's **Reset** button to restore it, or **Reset framing** to restore the camera controls together.

Driving input is held neutral only while keyboard/controller editing is enabled. The race continues running.

## Input viewer

Open **Input viewer** to enable the HUD and adjust its position, size and background opacity. The desktop image is a static layout preview; the in-game HUD shows live inputs.

**F9** shows or hides the input HUD. Your inputs take priority when racing against opponent ghosts. Enable recorded-input viewing if you want to see inputs while watching a replay.

## Finish or change shortcuts

Disable **Camera overrides**, click **Disconnect**, or close the app to return camera control to the game. Your settings remain saved for next time. Change F8/F9 in the Input viewer's **Shortcuts** section.

The desktop app stays available in the taskbar and Alt+Tab. Windows 10/11 x64 and the game's Direct3D 11 renderer are required. XInput-compatible controllers are supported.
